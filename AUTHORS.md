# Authors

WEFT is research software of the Printable Intelligence project at the
Faculty of Construction Management, University "Union – Nikola Tesla", Belgrade, Serbia.

- **Semir Poturak** — principal investigator; compiler design, the Refusing Gate, the printed specimens; corresponding author (poturaksemir@gmail.com)
- **Jelena Mitrović**
- **Bojan Končarević**
- **Matija Vračarić**
- **Tara Stojiljković**

The five are the authors of the paper *Printable Intelligence: The Leap into Micro-Structures and Tracing
Human-AI Fabrication* (STRAND / ON ARCHITECTURE 2026) and are credited jointly as the authors of this software.

Language models were used in two strictly separated capacities during development — as software engineers under
physical rules dictated by the human, and as bounded generative designers proposing parameters inside a contract —
and never wrote or edited machine instructions directly. Section 6 of the paper records the roles; the specimen
records in `specimens/` record what was printed.
