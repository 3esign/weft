# PREDICTIONS — ZIGURAT TKANJE · A2L · X1 (written 2026-09-17, before any print)

The A2L's bead (0.45 mm) is measured (D5, Šuma 2x2, everything since). The evidence base for the spans is
LIMIT16 (16 mm continuous), the OBLAK lintels 30/40/50 (held, sag unmeasured) and the GORA/OBLAK woven iris
(closed a 31 / 35.7 mm hole with straight chords). Nothing this size has been printed: 289 mm square,
160 mm tall, 1.74 km of thread, 2074 paths — about 1.75 x OBLAK.

The object is four concentric rounded-square tubes founded on the bed as one island (272.6 / 201.7 / 130.9 /
60.0 mm centreline sides), each ending 39 mm above the one outside it, with a woven terrace bridging each gap
(35.4 mm) and a hierarchical grid roof on the top tube. Every tier carries one grammar and a quilt of pillows
that scales with the tier (11 / 8 / 5 / 2 per face, two rows of 1.4 mm); the tiers turn 0, 15, 30 and 45
degrees (a quarter turn in all, accelerating upward). Hidden tube lengths (below the terrace of the tube
outside them) are plain staple at half the node density.

Falsifiers of the design: a corner of the outer brim lifting in the first hour (the object is then lost, not
measured — abort); a terrace-1 corner diagonal dropping and dragging its neighbours (the terrace becomes a
net — photograph, continue); the eight band of tier 4 unweaving. The roof's sag is the measurement, not a
failure: photograph each roof layer from the side with a rule against the top rim, then the finished roof
from above and below (through the tubes — a lamp under the plate).

What this print is for: the first WEFT object whose horizontal surfaces are built by the same rule as its
vertical ones (chords on chords, spans shrinking by a fixed ratio per level), and the first with nested bodies.
Every terrace is one gate-visible experiment (span 35 / 55), every roof level another (57 → 19 → 6 → 2).
