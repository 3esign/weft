## What was actually wrong, and how it was checked

Creality Print builds its preview layers in `LegacyRenderer.cpp`: a layer is
appended only when a move is classified `EMoveType::Extrude`, and a *decrease*
in Z appends a new layer just as an increase does. In `GCodeProcessor.cpp`,
every move between `; WIPE_START` and `; WIPE_END` is classified
`EMoveType::Wipe` instead.

The X1 Ender files placed the startup purge at Z 0.300 outside any wipe scope.
The renderer therefore made it layer 1 — two segments, a straight line from
(20, 2) to (130, 2) at the front edge of the bed — and the model's real base at
Z 0.200 became layer 2. That is the near-empty first layer seen in the slicer.
The base was never missing from the file.

Replaying that same rule over the file bytes:

| file | layer 1 Z | layer 1 segments | layer 1 extent | total layers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `DOMET_ENDER3V4_X1.gcode` | 0.300 | 2 | 20.0 – 130.0 × 2.0 – 2.0 | 279 |
| `DOMET_ENDER3V4_X2.gcode` | 0.200 | 1,371 | 53.7 – 166.3 × 53.7 – 166.3 | 278 |
| `DOMET2_ENDER3V4_X1.gcode` | 0.300 | 2 | 20.0 – 130.0 × 2.0 – 2.0 | 282 |
| `DOMET2_ENDER3V4_X2.gcode` | 0.200 | 1,231 | 59.7 – 160.3 × 59.7 – 160.3 | 281 |

`FIRST_LAYER_EVIDENCE.svg` in each Ender X2 folder draws both sides of that table.

The two Bambu files passed the same audit in X1 and in X2; their preview was
correct all along, which matches what was observed in the slicer.

The machine instructions did not change. The purge still runs at Z 0.300, with
the same extrusion and the same sequence. Only the comment markers around it
changed, so the renderer stops counting it as a model layer.
