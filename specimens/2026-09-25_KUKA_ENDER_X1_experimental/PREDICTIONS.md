# KUKA — predictions, written before the print

Stated so that the print can contradict them.

1. **`kuka` sectors (the 3 mm overshooting apex) curl; `zatvoren` sectors at the same 6 mm span do
   not.** This is the central prediction: the free END, not the free LENGTH, makes a hook.
2. **`rebro` is the flattest**: the rib ties every leg just laid and turns a row of teeth into a ring
   beam. If it is worse than `zatvoren`, the rib's own 9 mm bridges are the reason and the rib should
   be laid a layer later.
3. **`dupli` is stiffer than `zatvoren` but not flatter**: laying a tooth twice onto itself makes a
   blade, and the OBRTAJ evidence says blades stand; it does not say they lie flat.
4. **`koren` shows no visible difference from `zatvoren`** at 22 mm of reach. If it does, depth of
   anchoring matters even for corbels and it becomes a parameter.
5. **Each end condition matches its twin 180° away.** If not, the machine's airflow is a term.
6. **The drum is untouched whatever the terrace does.**
7. Nothing here predicts adhesion or that the print completes.
