# KUKA_ENDER_X1 — protocol

1. Print `KUKA_ENDER_X1.gcode` on the Creality Ender-3 V4. Change nothing: no fan, speed or temperature edits. The whole
   object is a comparison and every sector must see the same process.
2. Watch the first ten minutes for adhesion. The drum (18 mm) needs no attention after that.
3. **Watch the terrace from its first layer** (z 18 mm). If the nozzle begins to drag loosened
   strands, **stop the print**. The drum below is already a finished object and every sector that held
   is readable; nothing is lost by stopping, and the whole experiment is lost by letting a tangle run.
4. Photograph from directly above so all ten sectors are in one frame, then each sector from a fixed
   side with a scale (a ruler in the frame).
5. For each sector record: does the comb read as a comb; is the outer edge level, drooping, curled or
   tangled; does it match its twin 180° away. A rung that disagrees with its twin is a machine effect
   and must be written down as one.
6. Write the answers into `outcomes.md` in this folder as a NEW dated section; never rewrite an old one.
7. Sector 0 on the physical object: the sector that starts at the seam of the wall ring (0°); sectors
   ascend anticlockwise seen from above. `KUKA_ENDER_X1_plan.svg` is the map.
