## What this adds to WEFT

There is now local photographic evidence of this completed Creality ziggurat and its deformed internal strands. It is a baseline for the next horizontal-surface investigation. X2's corner-cut transitions rise across successive layers; they are **inclined ramps**, not the horizontal terraces required for the next experiments.

Semir's direction is to accept some sag and reduce it through successive changes to line routing, crossings, layer phase and inward/outward extension. The outer extension is a proposed counterweight; its mechanical benefit is not measured here. Upper tiers should load horizontal woven terraces, with only the first perimeter frame on the bed.
